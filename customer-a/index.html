Cost assumptions

Every click sends a live request to the configured routing engine (OSRM, ORS, or Google) for real driving distance & time from that point to all 5 sites. The popup lets you pick a line of business and a truck size independently — disposal cost scales with the truck's tonnage, while the max drive time cap comes from that specific line-of-business + truck-size combination (a 10 Ton Residential route and a 3 Ton Residential route can have different caps). If the cheapest site takes longer to drive to (one-way) than that combo's cap, its row is flagged instead of shown as optimal. Sites, lines of business, truck sizes and cost figures all come from the CONFIG block at the top of the script — edit the file to change those. Service area boundaries are drawn per site (any site can have none) purely as a visual reference — they don't affect which site is cheapest. Each boundary reflects whichever line-of-business + truck-size "basis" is picked above, fetched as a real road-routed isochrone (sampled drive time in a ring of directions around the site, not a straight-line radius); it shows as a plain circle first and upgrades once that fetch resolves, re-fetching whenever the basis changes, or staying a circle if the routing engine can't be reached. The return-trip, service-area, and basis controls above are the live controls, since they're things you'd flip case to case rather than reconfigure.
